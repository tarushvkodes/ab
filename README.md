Tarushv’s AB Tips 📘

Welcome to Tarushv’s AB Tips! This website offers valuable insights and resources to help you excel in AP Calculus AB. Whether you’re a student aiming for a high score or someone looking to refresh your knowledge, this site is designed to assist you.  ￼

Features 🌟
	•	Tips & Tricks: Practical advice to enhance your understanding and performance in AP Calculus AB.
	•	Resources: Curated links to official materials, online courses, and practice tools.
	•	Dark Mode Toggle: Switch between light and dark themes for a comfortable reading experience.

Technologies Used 🛠️
	•	HTML5: Structure and content of the website.
	•	CSS3: Styling and layout, including responsive design for various devices.
	•	JavaScript: Interactive elements like the dark mode toggle.

How to Use 📝
	1.	Access the Website: Open the index.html file in your web browser.
	2.	Navigate: Use the navigation bar to explore different sections:
	•	Tips & Tricks: Learn effective study strategies and concepts.
	•	Resources: Find links to official AP Calculus AB materials and practice tools.
	3.	Dark Mode: Click the “Toggle Dark Mode” link in the navigation bar to switch themes.
